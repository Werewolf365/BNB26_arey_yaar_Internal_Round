# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Software consumers and release operators who need to decide whether a release has sufficient, independent verification evidence before installing, promoting, or investigating it. They work with pinned commits, signed attestations, quorum rules, audit records, and optional chain anchors.

## Product Purpose

Quorum turns supply-chain verification evidence into a clear, bounded decision. It lets users inspect what was verified, what disagreed, what was excluded, and what remains insufficient.

## Brand Personality

Optimistic, precise, trustworthy. The interface should make serious evidence legible without looking punitive, cryptic, or like a generic security console.

## Anti-references

Avoid dark terminal-only security tools, generic blue SaaS dashboards, opaque trust scores, and decorative blockchain imagery that obscures the actual evidence.

## Design Principles

1. Make every decision traceable to visible evidence.
2. Preserve uncertainty and disagreement instead of smoothing it away.
3. Make safety-critical actions explicit and reversible where possible.
4. Give operational complexity a calm, readable structure.
5. Treat chain anchoring as verification infrastructure, not spectacle.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-navigation expectations. Respect reduced-motion preferences. Do not rely on color alone for verification states or alerts.
